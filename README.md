# ProfessorAte

<mark>Due to this project's scope, specific measures will be taken to make sure it adheres to the GDPR guidelines.</mark> The measures will change as the project progresses, but the guidelines will be upheld. See more about this [here](#gdpr).

## About

The goal of this project is to offer Portuguese students a platform to securely and safely rate professors they have attended classes from, so that other students can see how their methods are generally seen and rated by their peers.

### Features

This platform will allow users to rate professors on a series of categories in a given scale.  

You can then search a professor either by name, school or classes and see how your colleagues have been rating them over the years or their current tendency.

## GDPR

These constraints are always subject to change to better adhere to the GDPR.

- **No *written* personalized ratings are allowed**. 

- **No photos of professors or students are allowed to be shared**.

- **A valid university email will be necessary to be able to use the platform.**

- **Professors that want to object to being listed can do so easily and will be removed**

We believe this platform would centralize student feedback and is of legitimate interest to the student population, with the processing of professors' personal data being very limited, and only used for this purpose.
